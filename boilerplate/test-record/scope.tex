\subsection{Scope}
This \stdRptPkg\ applies to \Gls{company} medical device software projects that
require verification testing for a \stdCfgItem.  It covers the activities,
records, and conclusions used to determine whether the \stdCfgItem\ satisfies
the applicable system requirements.

\tlcVspace

The scope should identify the tested configuration, included requirements,
excluded requirements, test environment, required tools, and any limitations that
affect interpretation of the verification conclusion.
