\subsection{Purpose}
The purpose of this \stdDesignReview\ is to document a structured review of the
specified software design information before it is used for subsequent lifecycle
activities.  The review evaluates whether the design is sufficiently complete,
traceable, testable, and controlled to support implementation, verification, and
validation planning.

\tlcVspace

The completed record supports medical device software governance by preserving
the review rationale, decisions, action items, and approvals needed by software,
quality, and regulatory stakeholders.
