\subsection{Scope}
This \stdDesignReview\ applies to the software design information identified in
this report, including the design inputs, design outputs, interfaces, risk
controls, assumptions, and unresolved issues that are relevant to the review
decision.  The scope should identify any excluded items and explain why they do
not affect the review conclusion.

\tlcVspace

The review does not replace required verification, validation, risk management,
or configuration management activities.  It provides evidence that the design was
examined at an appropriate lifecycle point and that follow-up actions were
assigned where additional control was required.
