\subsection{Scope}
This \stdRptPkg\ applies to \Gls{company} medical device software projects that
require validation of a \stdCfgItem\ for its intended use.  It covers the
activities, records, and conclusions used to determine whether the \stdCfgItem\
satisfies its applicable \stdIUR.

\tlcVspace

The scope should identify the validated configuration, intended use,
environment, required tools, input data, excluded uses, and limitations that
affect interpretation of the validation conclusion.
