\section{Conclusion}
Replace this boilerplate with the report-specific conclusion supported by the
documented evidence.  The conclusion should state the decision reached, whether
the acceptance criteria were satisfied, and any residual limitations, open items,
or follow-up actions.

\tlcVspace

When the report supports a regulated medical device software activity, the
conclusion should be written so software teams, quality authorities, and
regulatory reviewers can trace the decision back to the evidence and understand
its impact on design control, risk management, verification, validation, or
release readiness.
